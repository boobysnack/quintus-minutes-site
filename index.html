Introduction

Quintus Minutes (“the App”) is a fully on-device lecture, meeting and personal private recorder. It records audio, produces live transcripts and summaries, and keeps everything in your own hands.

Key points
  • All data — recordings, transcripts, summaries, settings — is stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage.
  • No data is ever sent to external servers by the App itself.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no third-party analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no telemetry.
  • Transcription, summarization and speaker labeling all run locally on your device using embedded AI models.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 The App itself collects nothing

Quintus Minutes transmits none of your data anywhere. Specifically:

  • Audio recordings — stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. Never uploaded.
  • Transcripts and summaries — generated on-device by the embedded AI models. Never uploaded.
  • Usage data — none. The App contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting service, and no advertising components.
  • Contacts, location, photos, files — the App does not request or use these permissions at all.

1.2 Voice embeddings (speaker labeling)

The App includes a built-in speaker-labeling feature. After a recording session ends, it automatically works out which parts of the audio belong to which speaker, so transcripts can carry speaker names (e.g. “Speaker 1”). This computation is part of the recording pipeline and runs locally for every session; the corresponding model files are already built into the App itself.

For this labeling, short voice embeddings — numeric fingerprints used only to group speech segments by speaker — are computed on your device:

  • Embeddings are held only in memory while the current session’s speaker labels are being computed.
  • They are never persisted, never written to disk, and never transmitted.
  • When labeling of the session completes — or the session is deleted — the embeddings are discarded immediately. Only the resulting speaker labels are stored with the transcript, locally on your device.

The labeling computation itself is part of the recording pipeline and cannot be switched off; however, you can hide speaker names at any time using the “Show speakers / Hide speakers” control in the session detail — hiding also causes the session’s summaries to be rebuilt without speaker references, all locally.

1.3 Microphone

The microphone is used only while a recording session is active (or paused-but-open, as indicated by the recording indicator on screen). Recording stops entirely when you stop the session.

2. Third-Party Downloads (model files)

On first setup, the App downloads static, openly published AI model files so that transcription and summarization can run fully offline afterward:

  • Hugging Face — the transcription models (Whisper) and, optionally, the summarization model (official Gemma model)
  • GitHub Releases — the voice-activity-detection model and additional transcription model files

The speaker-labeling models are already built into the App itself and require no download.

During these one-time downloads, the hosting server sees standard network information that any download involves (your IP address, download request). No content of your recordings or sessions is ever part of these requests. Every downloaded file is verified by SHA-256 checksum before use. After setup, the App works completely offline — you can turn on Airplane Mode right after setup and everything keeps running.

4. AI-Generated Content

Transcripts and summaries in this App are generated automatically by on-device AI models. Like all AI output, they can contain mistakes — misheard words, omitted statements, or wrong speaker attributions.

  • Always verify critical content (medical, legal, financial, contractual) against the original recording. The App does not replace professional advice.
  • To report inaccurate, offensive, or otherwise problematic generated content, contact us (Section 8). Sending the report remains under your control — the App does not transmit your recordings or content any further on its own.

5. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and contains no ads, no social features, and no data collection of any kind — including from children.

6. Legal Basis

We process no personal data on any server, so there is no server-side processing for which a separate legal basis would be required. All data processing described above happens solely on your device, under your control (access to own-device data is not in scope of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”)). Third-party model downloads (Section 2) are strictly necessary for the App’s core function.
